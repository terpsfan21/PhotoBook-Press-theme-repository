## Rules that apply to everything

- **Style:** as the test set. Warm gouache and paper-cut texture, clear silhouettes, quiet backgrounds; cream, muted teal, ochre, coral, leaf green.
- **File names:** exactly as written here. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces, no "final" or "v2". The editor finds files by name.
- **No words in the art.** No titles, captions or labels. A sign with a tooth or scissors symbol is fine. All story text is added by the editor.
- **The child is never painted into a background.**
- **Delivery:** zip per batch, all files in one folder.
- **With each batch, tell me:** the tool used and that commercial use is allowed; the size each image was generated at and whether it was enlarged; anything that does not meet this brief.

**Owner decision before starting Package A:** the test child has a large head and small body and reads younger than the 4–9 age range and the original character board. Confirm with the owner whether to keep these proportions or move toward the board. Whichever is chosen must be used for every pose.

## Package A — character layers

### Canvas (same as the test)

- 1500 × 2250 px PNG, true transparency, no glow, halo or shadow.
- The point on the ground between the feet is at **x 750, y 2100** in every file of every pose.
- Standing: top of the bare head at about y 600. Seated poses are shorter; keep the feet on the same anchor point.
- All layers of one pose must line up exactly when stacked.

### The six poses

| Pose name | Description |
|---|---|
| `stand` | Standing, facing the viewer, arms relaxed at the sides |
| `wave` | Standing, one arm raised in a wave (**started in the test set**) |
| `handUp` | Standing, one arm straight up with an open palm: the "pause" signal |
| `sit` | Sitting upright on an invisible seat, facing the viewer, knees forward, lower legs hanging down, hands resting on the lap |

Seated poses are placed onto chairs painted in the backgrounds (waiting-room chair, dental chair, salon chair), so draw no chair in the character layers. Keep the three seated poses identical from the waist down so the same chair fits all three.

## Package B — page backgrounds

### Specification (same as the test)

- 2625 × 2625 px JPEG, quality 90 or higher, sRGB. Art runs to every edge; the outer 38 px each side is trimmed in print.
- **Bottom 26% calm** (floor or ground only) on every story page. The read-aloud text sits there.
- Keep faces and important detail at least 115 px from every edge.
- File name: `nub-<book>-p<NN>-bg.jpg`, covers `nub-<book>-cover-front-bg.jpg` and `nub-<book>-cover-back-bg.jpg`. `<book>` is `dentist` or `haircut`.

### How to read the page lists

- **Child:** the pose the editor will place, and roughly where. "x 40, y 70" means 40% from the left and the feet 70% from the top. Height is the child's height as a share of the picture. Leave that spot clear. Positions are approximate; I adjust them to fit your painting.
- **Seated pages:** paint the chair empty, facing the viewer, with a clear flat seat. The seat should be where the child's hips will be.
- **Picture cards:** on "card" pages, paint large, simple picture cards (cream rounded squares with one object each). No words on the cards.
- **Other people** are painted into the background and are the same person on every page they appear.

### The people

| Who | Look |
|---|---|
| Dentist | As painted in the test page 22: white coat over teal scrubs, friendly |
| Dental helper | A second team member in teal scrubs |
| Front-desk person | Seated or standing behind a reception desk |
| Stylist | Friendly hair stylist in a dark apron with a comb in the pocket |
| The grown-up | **Owner decision needed.** The child's look is chosen by the family, but a painted grown-up has one fixed look. Recommended: show the grown-up only from behind or cropped at the shoulders, in a cream sweater with long sleeves, so no face or skin tone is fixed. Use the same treatment on every page |

This page list follows the current draft of each story. **If you rewrite a story, send me the new page list first** (page number, words, what the picture shows, pose, where the child goes) so the editor and the art stay in step.

## What I do with each batch

I drop the files in, run the checker, and send back a sheet showing every combination on real pages, with a list of anything that is the wrong size, misnamed, or does not line up. Nothing goes to print until a physical proof of each book has been checked.
