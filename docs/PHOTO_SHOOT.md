# Product photos and flip-through video

The home page and product pages are built around photos of the real printed books. Every photo has a named slot. Until a file with that name exists, the slot shows a mockup made from the real page art, tagged "Mockup · photo to come". Drop a photo into `content/photos/` with the right name and it replaces the mockup; no code changes.

## What to print first

One bound Linemark proof of each launch book in each cover type: four books. Use one example character for all four so the photos match (suggested: the default character, with glasses), and a short, clear name on the cover.

## Shot list

All stills: landscape 4:3, at least 2400 × 1800 px, JPEG, sRGB. Soft daylight, a plain warm surface (wood or cream), no props that compete with the book. Keep the whole book inside the frame with a little room around it.

## Before the shoot

- A signed photo release from a parent or guardian for any child who appears, even hands only.
- Shoot the hardcover and softcover of the same title in the same light, in one session.
- Take a few extra angles of each; social posts and ads can use what the site does not.
