# Next Up Books artwork

The scene board shows dentist, haircut, school and airplane, in reading order. The character board illustrates possible appearance and support selections. Both are flat art direction references.

For a layered prototype, draw classroom-background.png first. Place classroom-child-cutout.png on top at about 45% of scene height with the character's feet near 87% of the scene height and the figure center near 33% of the scene width. Keep the name and story copy as separate editable text. The child PNG has transparency. Its hair, clothing, glasses and backpack are drawn together in this example.

The artwork was generated as concept art and storefront prototype material. It contains one separate child pose and one background plate. Production requires new full-resolution assets, interchangeable choices and poses for each scene, and proofs for the 8.5 × 8.5 inch trim in both bindings. Use the included business plan and build brief for scope.
